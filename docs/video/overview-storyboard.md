# Automatic monthly billing with UPI AutoPay - script and storyboard

Video: `docs/video/out/overview/overview.mp4` - 8 scenes, about 3 min 07 s. Voice: Microsoft Zira Desktop. Everything below can be re-recorded by a person by reading the narration.

## Scene 1: Automatic monthly billing with UPI AutoPay

- **Time:** 0:00 - 0:17 (17 s)
- **On screen:** Title card on a dark background with the product name.

> This is a short overview of the new automatic monthly billing for AI Sales Automation. Tenants can now pay for their plan through UPI AutoPay. They approve once in their UPI app, and Razorpay takes the payment every month after that.

## Scene 2: How a tenant subscribes

- **Time:** 0:17 - 0:43 (26 s)
- **On screen:** Five numbered steps left to right, each with a short caption.

> The tenant chooses a plan and presses Subscribe. They are taken to Razorpay, where they approve a mandate in their UPI app, such as Google Pay. Once Razorpay confirms the first payment, the plan starts and the plan's credits are added to the wallet. After that, Razorpay takes each month's payment by itself. The tenant does nothing more.

## Scene 3: What the tenant sees on the Billing screen

- **Time:** 0:43 - 1:07 (23 s)
- **On screen:** Four cards describing the Billing screen.

> On the Billing screen the tenant sees the state of their automatic payment, the monthly amount, and the date of the next payment. They can cancel whenever they like, after a confirmation, and they keep what they have already paid for. Their payment history shows every attempt, including failed ones with the reason, and each invoice.

## Scene 4: Three promises that keep money and credits correct

- **Time:** 1:07 - 1:37 (30 s)
- **On screen:** Three large cards, one per promise.

> Three promises matter most. First, the price is always ours. It comes from our database, never from the browser. Second, a plan starts only when Razorpay confirms the payment. A tenant coming back to our page proves nothing. Third, credits are added once per payment. Razorpay sometimes repeats its messages, and a repeat never adds credits twice.

## Scene 5: When a payment fails

- **Time:** 1:37 - 2:01 (24 s)
- **On screen:** Four steps from a failed payment to the free plan.

> If a payment fails, Razorpay tries again over the next three days. The plan keeps working through four grace days, and the tenant is told what to do. If the payment never goes through, the tenant moves back to the free plan. Credits already in their wallet stay theirs. A failed payment never adds credits.

## Scene 6: Built and checked

- **Time:** 2:01 - 2:22 (21 s)
- **On screen:** Three big numbers and a caution line.

> The work is built in seven phases. All two thousand eight hundred and one backend tests pass, and all one thousand five hundred and ninety three screen tests pass. To be clear, those tests use a stand-in for Razorpay. Nothing has yet been run against Razorpay itself.

## Scene 7: What is still needed

- **Time:** 2:22 - 2:55 (33 s)
- **On screen:** Five rows: what must happen before launch.

> Before launch, a few things are needed. Razorpay must switch on Subscriptions and UPI for our account. We then run the written test checklist in test mode, and finally one small real mandate, all the way through its second monthly payment. Two decisions are open: the four grace days, and whether Razorpay's invoice can be used as the tax invoice. Refunds, credit pack payments and switching between paid plans are not built yet.

## Scene 8: Safe to switch on, step by step

- **Time:** 2:55 - 3:07 (12 s)
- **On screen:** Closing card.

> The feature is off by default, so nothing changes for any tenant until it is switched on. The next step is the test checklist. Thank you.
